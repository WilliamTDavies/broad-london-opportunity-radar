How roles qualify

The scanner searches official sources and employer-agnostic boards. Roles with explicit conflicts are removed; the rest are labelled verified or possible according to the available eligibility evidence.

Useful limits

No scraper can guarantee every vacancy on the web. “Possible” is intentionally permissive, not an acceptance prediction: confirm degree, study year, work rights, pay and deadline on the application page.
